\section{Application Overview}

\subsection{Purpose and Scope}

The Smart Parking Assistant targets urban commuters and drivers navigating the challenges of on-street parking. It addresses key issues such as the lack of centralised parking information, confusing signage, time-limit fines, and uncertainty about space availability.

Existing solutions are limited: government meters offer little digital integration, while private apps cover only select commercial lots. Most on-street parking remains invisible to digital systems. Our app bridges this gap through a comprehensive, user-maintained database covering public parking spaces.

Using a collaborative model, users scan signs, confirm locations, and track parking sessions, collectively building a live map of parking restrictions. This crowdsourced approach keeps data accurate and expands naturally as users explore new areas.

\subsection{Key Features}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Interactive Parking Map} (Figure~\ref{fig:map_screen}): The core feature presents a geospatial view of parking locations with pins indicating parking restrictions by day of the week and time of day. Users can filter results based on their parking duration needs and search for suitable spots before their journey. The map displays crucial information including time limits (e.g., 2-hour parking), restricted hours (e.g., no parking 4-6 PM), and day-specific variations. This allows drivers to plan their parking strategy before reaching their destination.
    
    \item \textbf{AI-Powered Sign Recognition} (Figure~\ref{fig:camera_screen}): Parking signage can be complex. Our Gemini-powered scanning feature allows users to photograph parking signs and receive instant, plain-language interpretations of the restrictions, with reference to the user's location and current time and day. The AI extracts key information such as time limits and restricted periods. Critically, this information is then contributed to our crowdsourced database, continuously improving the accuracy and coverage of parking restriction data across the city.
    
    \item \textbf{Automated Parking Timer} (Figure~\ref{fig:map_screen}): Traditional parking timers rely on users to manually set and estimate their duration. Our solution automates this process by detecting when a user has parked (using GPS, gyroscope, accelerometer and bluetooth sensors) and prompting for confirmation. Upon confirmation, the app automatically retrieves the applicable parking restrictions for that specific location, day, and time, then initiates a countdown timer. Users also have the option to customize the timer to suit their needs. Push notifications warn users 5 minutes before their parking time expires.
    
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Availability Information} (Figure~\ref{fig:map_screen}): Knowing parking availability in advance saves time and reduces congestion. In the City of Melbourne areas with official API data, the app visualises availability using colour segments (orange to red, with red indicating busier zones). In other regions, it estimates availability from anonymised user location data and weights it by segment size to show relative parking demand compared to the global busiest segment. This crowdsourced availability data helps users choose less congested areas before arrival.
    
\end{enumerate}
